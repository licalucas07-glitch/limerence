📖 Audit Metrics Guide

Low-Effort Morning Check-ins

Short greetings (<=5 words) sent between 6am-10am. Measures superficial consistency vs. meaningful engagement.

Topic Carrying Score

Percentage of times a participant sent follow-up messages or asked questions to keep the chat alive after a morning opener.

Calls Initiated vs. Returned

Proactive calls initiated out of the blue versus calls returned after missing an incoming call prompt.

Calls Placed & Missed

Shows outbound calls placed by a participant that went unanswered by the other person, with exact names specified.

Avg Call Return Speed

Average time (in minutes) it takes a participant to call back after missing an incoming call from the other person.

Longest Silent Streak

The longest continuous dead gap in the chat, identifying who sent the last message and who finally reached back out.
